Shubh Deepawali

Wishing You & Your Beloved Family An Abundance of Light, Joy & Divine Grace

तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय ।

Dear Beloved Friends & Family,

On this auspicious festival of lights, may the radiant glow of a thousand golden diyas dispel every shadow of doubt and illuminate your path with boundless optimism, prosperity, and laughter.

Just as Bhagwan Rama returned to Ayodhya amidst golden celebrations, may victorious new beginnings, good health, and joyful reunions fill your home today and in all the years ahead.

With warmest blessings & heartfelt prayers, Your Well-Wishers Deepawali • Samvat 2083

The Four Blessings of Deepawali

🌿

Aarogya (Radiant Health)

May you and your family be blessed with sound vitality, peaceful minds, rejuvenating energy, and long fulfilling lives.

✦ ✦ ✦
🪙

Samriddhi (Abundance)

✦ ✦ ✦
🐘

Siddhi (Wisdom & Joy)

May Lord Ganesha remove every obstacle on your journey, granting clarity, auspicious wisdom, and effortless victory.

✦ ✦ ✦

Shanti (Harmony & Love)

May your home resonate with warmth, unbreakable family affection, laughter, and serene tranquility throughout the year.

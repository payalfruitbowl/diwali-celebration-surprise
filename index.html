A Glimpse Into Our Home

How We Celebrated Our Diwali

🪔✨🪔

From the chaotic Dhanteras cleaning to midnight sparklers and devouring boxes of Kaju Katli — take a walk through our festivities!

🪙
Day 1 • Dhanteras
🏺✨
🪙🪙🪙🪙🪙

The Great Deep-Cleaning & Gold Shopping

Mom inspected every corner of the house with a laser eye! We finally bought our shiny brass utensil and traditional silver coin for Lakshmi ji, followed by a hearty dinner of piping hot samosas.

“Everything must sparkle before Lakshmi enters!” — Dadi
🎨
Day 2 • Choti Diwali
🪷

Courtyard Rangoli & 14 Diyas

Three hours of bent backs, seven vibrant powder colors, and an accidental footprint from the neighbor's cat! But our Peacock Rangoli turned out to be the talk of the entire neighborhood street.

🪔
Day 3 • The Main Night
🪔🙏🪔

The Sacred Lakshmi-Ganesh Puja & Feast

Draped in brand-new silk kurtas and Banarasi sarees, our entire family gathered for the evening Aarti. We filled 101 clay diyas with mustard oil and cotton wicks, placing them on every ledge, window, and roof parapet.

The feast: Paneer makhani, fragrant saffron poori, dry fruit kheer, and grandma's secret warm gulab jamuns.

🎆
Midnight Celebration
🎇💥

Laughter, Green Crackers & Terrace Sparklers

The sky turned purple and gold! The kids were spinning phuljhadis making glowing light spirals in long-exposure photos while cousins fought over who gets to light the last rocket.

#TerraceNights#PhuljhadiFun#FamilyTogether
🍬
Day 5 • Bhai Dooj
🎁🥮

The Sweet Exchange & Bidding Blessings

Brother-sister rituals, affectionate teasing, exchanging mithai boxes (and passing the mysterious Soan Papdi box to the next relative's house!). Wrapping up 5 unforgettable days of pure joy.

Kuch Meetha Ho Jaye!

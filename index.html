A Glimpse Into Our Home

How We Celebrated Our Diwali

🪔✨🪔

From the chaotic Dhanteras cleaning to midnight sparklers and devouring boxes of Kaju Katli — take a walk through our festivities!

🪙
✦ Day 1 • Dhanteras & Prosperity ✦
AUSPICIOUS BEGINNINGS

The Great Deep-Cleaning & Royal Gold Shopping

Mom inspected every corner of the house with a laser eye! We welcomed Goddess Lakshmi with glowing brass Kalash, 24K gold ornaments, and consecrated silver coins, followed by a hearty feast of hot samosas and saffron jalebis.

“When the house is clean and hearts are pure, Lakshmi resides forever.” — Dadi
🎨
✦ Day 2 • Choti Diwali & Rangoli ✦
SACRED ARTISTRY

Courtyard Peacock Rangoli & 14 Diyas

Three hours of bent backs, seven vibrant mineral colors, and an accidental footprint from the neighbor's cat! Our grand Peacock & Lotus Mandala surrounded by 14 glowing mustard-oil diyas became the showstopper of the entire street.

“Every flower and color is a prayer of welcome.” — Maa
🪔
✦ Day 3 • The Sacred Lakshmi Puja ✦
THE HOLY NIGHT

Sacred Lakshmi-Ganesh Aarti & Royal Feast

Draped in pure Banarasi silk sarees and brocade kurtas, our three generations gathered for the evening Aarti. We consecrated 101 clay diyas along every terrace parapet and windowsill, filling the home with celestial light.

✨ The Royal Feast: Shahi paneer makhani, fragrant saffron puris, dry fruit rabdi, and piping hot gulab jamuns.

🎆
✦ Day 4 • Midnight Terrace Celebration ✦
NIGHT OF A THOUSAND STARS

Terrace Sparklers, Green Crackers & Joy

The night sky erupted into cascades of gold and amethyst! Grandparents cheered from wicker chairs while kids painted glowing light spirals with burning phuljhadis, creating memories etched forever.

#TerraceNights#PhuljhadiMagic#FamilyTogether
🍬
✦ Day 5 • Bhai Dooj & Sweet Gifting ✦
SWEET BONDS OF LOVE

Bhai Dooj Tikka & The Royal Mithai Exchange

Brother-sister blessings, sacred roli tikka rituals, playful teasing, and trading silver-leafed Kaju Katli boxes (along with the legendary Soan Papdi pass-around!). A sweet culmination of five magical festive days.

“May the sweetness of this Diwali stay with us all year round.” — Papa
